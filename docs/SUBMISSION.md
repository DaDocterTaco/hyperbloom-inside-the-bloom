# Devpost handoff

**Project:** Hyperbloom — Inside the Bloom

**One-line description:** A cinematic redesign that turns a hackathon website into one unfolding bloom, while keeping every path clear and usable.

**Live website:** https://hyperbloom-bloom-2026.web.app/

**Source code:** https://github.com/DaDocterTaco/hyperbloom-inside-the-bloom

## What it does

The redesign brings the original Hyperbloom content into a continuous, responsive experience. A layered glass flower opens as visitors scroll. The current challenge leads directly into genuine partners, support for student builders, real community evidence, the event archive, journal, team and contact paths. Dedicated blog and gallery pages carry the same materials and reading clarity.

## How it was built

React, Vite, CSS and position-based requestAnimationFrame motion. The work combines an editorial reading column with independently moving glass layers, an accessible partner ribbon and responsive navigation. Bloom assets were created with ImageGen and optimized to WebP. Phosphor supplies icons; fonts are self-hosted. Firebase Hosting serves the compiled frontend with SPA route rewrites.

## What to demonstrate

1. Open the homepage and scroll naturally through the bloom into the challenge and partner ribbon.

3. Open Blog, search for API, and enter the local reading guide. Point out the complete original article link.
4. Open Gallery and the genuine V1 winner and video recordings.
5. Show the phone layout and the motion preference.
